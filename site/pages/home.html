General Agent Framework — Governed AI Actions

Build AI agents that can safely act on real business systems.

Human approval. Durable execution. UNKNOWN-aware reconciliation. Auditable external actions.

“Safely” is a design goal. It means acting under explicit operator-defined governance, approval, evidence, recovery and audit controls. It is not a security, legal, regulatory or compliance certification. No universal exactly-once guarantee or guaranteed third-party idempotency is implied.

This public showcase contains documentation and demos. The commercial implementation remains private and separately licensed.

Four control concerns

An action needs more than a good suggestion

01

Authorization & approval

Examine the proposal and bind the approval to the intended action. Keep requester and approver responsibilities separate.

02

Durable intent

Record an intent and execution attempt before an external call, so an interrupted operation leaves evidence.

03

Honest uncertainty

A lost response can leave an action UNKNOWN. An uncertain write must not be blindly repeated.

04

Evidence & audit

Keep decisions and observations inspectable. Approval-task chain verification has a specific scope.

Conceptual concerns illustrated by the technical refund demo; the manufacturing simulation shows business behavior and governance boundaries, not commercial execution.
